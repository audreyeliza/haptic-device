# Project Journal

**1-DOF Molecular Force Feedback Haptic Device**

## Spring 26 Semester Write Up

Our group was presented with the goal of creating a haptic device with the
ability to move, rotate, provide force resistance feedback, and maintain
position on an atom or molecule in any direction within a 3 dimensional
digital space. We began by examining the old haptic device, which only
provided 3 DOF, and began prototyping low-resolution models with 1 dimension
of movement, with the intent of scaling once we completed a working
prototype.

We ended the semester with a design that follows a unidirectional
cable/belt drive system, which as of right now has one major limitation in
my perspective, as it can only render forces in one direction actively, so
anything pushing back is passive. Some alternatives to our current design
include an antagonistic actuation (or dual motor) approach similar to the
medical robotics and haptics FRI device, or if we stick with one motor, a
capstan drive.

My contributions included creating physical and digital CAD prototype
models and comparing and researching components to flesh out the
functionality and mechanics of the device itself and items for our shopping
list.
